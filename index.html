02 // The autonomy scale

L4 today. Building toward L5.

The distinction is who carries the engagement — and when a human steps in.

  1. L0 Manual A human runs every tool and interprets every result.
  2. L1 Assisted Scanners flag candidates. A human verifies each one.
  3. L2 Partial automation Scripted checks. A human drives the engagement.
  4. L3 Supervised autonomy AI executes tasks. A human approves each step.
  5. Saifullah today L4 High autonomy The full engagement. A human reviews the output.
  6. Not achieved L5 Full autonomy Novel situations. No human involvement.
